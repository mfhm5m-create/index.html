        How to Play

        Goal

        Deduce the secret Suspect, Weapon, and Room hidden in the case file before your rivals — or before everyone runs out of guesses.

        On Your Turn

        • Roll the dice and move through corridors into any reachable room, or take a Secret Passage from a corner room straight to its diagonal partner.
        • If you're in a room, you may make a Suggestion: name a suspect and weapon (the room is whichever you're in). That suspect's and the weapon's tokens glide into the room with you.
        • Other investigators are asked — in turn order — whether they hold any of the three cards. The first one who can, privately reveals just one card to you.
        • At any point on your turn you may make a formal Accusation naming suspect + weapon + room. Guess right and you win instantly. Guess wrong and you're out as an active investigator — though you stay on the board to disprove others.

        The Notebook

        Use the Sleuth's Notebook to track what's been ruled out. Cards in your own hand are auto-checked.

        Modes

        Local Pass & Play hides each hand between turns with a hand-off screen. Online uses direct peer-to-peer connections (PeerJS) — one player hosts and shares a room code.
