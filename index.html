        How to Play — Classic Rules

        Goal

        Deduce the three cards in the Case File: one Suspect, one Weapon, and one Room.

        Setup & Turn Order

        • Classic play is for 3–6 investigators. One card from each category is hidden in the Case File; all remaining cards are dealt.
        • All six suspect tokens remain on the board, even when fewer than six people play. The six weapons begin in six different rooms.
        • Miss Scarlett has first priority in classic turn order; play then continues around the table.

        Movement

        • Roll one die. Move horizontally or vertically, never diagonally. You cannot enter the same corridor square twice during the same turn.
        • You cannot move through or land on a corridor square occupied by another token. Entering a room ends movement immediately.
        • If you begin your turn in a corner room, you may use its Secret Passage instead of rolling.

        Suggestions

        • After entering a room, you may make one Suggestion for that room: name one Suspect and one Weapon. Both tokens are moved into your room.
        • Starting with the next investigator in turn order, the first person able to disprove must privately reveal exactly one matching card of their choice.
        • You may include cards from your own hand in a Suggestion. You may also make a Suggestion and an Accusation on the same turn.
        • You cannot simply remain in a room and make the same room's Suggestion again next turn. If another player's Suggestion moves your token into a room, on your next turn you may Suggest there without rolling.

        Accusations

        On your turn you may accuse any Suspect, Weapon, and Room. A correct Accusation wins. A wrong Accusation removes you from movement, Suggestions, and future Accusations, but you still privately disprove other players' Suggestions when able.

        Detective Notebook

        The notebook is private to each investigator. Your own cards, public “could not disprove” information, and cards privately shown to you are tracked automatically. You can add your own ✕, ?, and ✔ marks.
